The ASCII artwork in flame.txt is sampled from the netfyr design library's
original flame and its SVG animation. It is a terminal rendering of the mark,
not a replacement master. The netfyr name and standalone mark remain all rights
reserved; this repository's MIT software license does not license the mark.

Source files and their SHA-256 hashes are recorded in flame-provenance.json.
Regenerate from the netfyr-design repository root:
  node tui/scripts/generate-flame.mjs
Verify the generated assets:
  node tui/scripts/generate-flame.mjs --check
